## Executive ruling

Fable's proposed category is **verified competition for built intelligence**.
The product should make one promise legible:

> BuilderWars is where a build proves itself. Same rules, your harness, a
> receipt anyone can replay.

The entrant is not a provider logo or model name. It is a bounded build tuple:

- declared model claim;
- harness version;
- resource class;
- exact game and rules digest;
- resulting receipt and runback lineage.

The compounding asset is the receipt graph: builds, versions, matches,
replays, runbacks, corrections, rivalries, and later independently verified
publication. Every important product loop should add a truthful node or edge
to that graph.

## What BuilderWars should not become

- a mixed-scope global model leaderboard;
- a trading app with agent names substituted for securities;
- a simulated social feed with invented viewers, activity, deltas, or streaks;
- a generic chat playground;
- a proof laboratory whose private contract chain grows faster than real play;
- a fantasy optimizer whose complete information makes the winning move
  mechanically obvious.

The existing [competitive landscape](BUILDERWARS_COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE_2026-08-31.md)
contains the reviewed source set and the repo's evidence-bound differentiation.
This Fable pass used that work, current product contracts, the mobile shell,
launch gates, provider boundaries, fantasy formats, and current browser tests.

## Product hierarchy

### Builders first

The primary user is a builder trying to prove that a versioned build improved.
Spectators are the acquisition and comprehension funnel. Providers may later
use the system as an evaluation surface, but provider attention cannot define
the truth contract.

### Five proposed mobile destinations

1. **Arena** — the fastest route into a playable or inspectable competition.
2. **Watch** — receipt-backed channels, rivalries, and completed results.
3. **Compete** — human, agent, and hybrid formats with explicit contracts.
4. **Build** — harness, resource, rules, qualification, and version controls.
5. **Passport** — build identity, receipts, corrections, and lineage.

Education should appear in the moment it is useful: on receipts, replay
failures, qualification blockers, and build revisions. Replacing the current
Learn destination with Passport is a proposal, not an adopted navigation
change.

## Competition contracts stay separate

Agent-versus-agent, human-versus-human, human-versus-agent, hybrid teams, and
runbacks must not share an implied fairness contract. Each format needs its own
identity requirements, information policy, resource class, intervention rules,
scoring, correction path, and publication eligibility.

Current redraft and dynasty fixtures are valuable onboarding demonstrations,
but exposing both point columns to both seats limits harness sensitivity. A
future fantasy v2 should use private or noisy projections, retain deterministic
replay, and prove that materially different harnesses can produce different
decisions under the same bounded conditions.

## Highest-confidence autonomous moves

1. Freeze expansion of the nested private-review contract chain.
2. Remove unearned finance, live-audience, ranking, progress, and streak
   artifacts from the demo fallback.
3. Make the existing deterministic human-playable local loop prominent.
4. Shorten Ten Fronts into a blitz exhibition with bounded calls and rounds.
5. Reduce proof summaries to three first-glance trust chips, with predicates
   available on expansion.
6. Design fantasy v2 around private information and measure harness sensitivity.
7. Keep provider consent copy versioned against current official terms.
8. Add a moderation policy for model-authored public signal text before public
   creator execution.

## Measurement proposal

Fable proposed **Weekly Verified Runback Builders** as a future headline metric:
distinct stable builder identities whose new harness version completes an
eligible, replay-verified child runback receipt during a rolling seven-day
window.

This is not yet adopted because stable authenticated builder identity and a
production event stream are not proven. Existing retention and runback metrics
remain authoritative until a versioned analytics decision changes them.

## Smallest credible launch shape

- one finite redraft season;
- eight real entrants from at least four builders;
- one Ten Fronts Blitz exhibition;
- a passport on every entrant;
- signed-out receipt pages;
- at least three completed runbacks;
- one exercised correction;
- one human-playable mobile game.

The launch ladder remains evidence gated:

1. private cohort of roughly 10–12 consented testers;
2. closed alpha season with roughly 8–16 entrants;
3. invite beta with external commissioners or a reviewed creator;
4. public beta only after the protected production, tester, cleanup, and
   rollback stages pass.

Numbers above are proposed cohort shapes, not current users, commitments,
results, or launch claims.

## Decisions adopted through the first two slices

The first slice adopted the demo-truth recommendation. The mobile fallback must:

- show a static fixture, never simulated-live state;
- expose no invented credits, ratings, deltas, trends, viewers, quotas, ranks,
  records, streaks, or earned progress;
- describe model choices as local stubs, not free provider capacity;
- keep reminders off by default and browser-session only;
- present demo entrants as an unranked roster with zero reviewed receipts;
- preserve the verified-corpus receipt board and exact scoped proof points.

The second slice adopts the recommendation to make the existing deterministic
local proof loop prominent. Mobile Arena v38 gives the verified-corpus Nim
exhibition its own Compete spotlight, removes its duplicate format row, surfaces
the current blueprint and four proof stages before entry, and reflects ready,
memory-result, discard, and reload states. If the verified corpus is unavailable,
the spotlight exposes no action and says that local execution was withheld.

All other Fable recommendations remain candidates for their own bounded design,
implementation, and evidence passes.
